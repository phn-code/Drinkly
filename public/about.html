Our Mission

Drinkly is your personal guide to cocktails, homebrewing, and beverage discovery. Whether you're mixing a martini or crafting a ginger beer, we help you track, review, and share your drink experiences with friends.

Why We Built This

We believe that drinks bring people together. With so many amazing recipes and stories out there, we wanted a single space to explore, save, and celebrate all things liquid and delicious.

Meet the Team

This website was created by a group of Computer Science students at the University of Adelaide. We're developers, designers, and drink enthusiasts who wanted to blend our passions into something meaningful. Cheers to community, creativity, and collaboration!
